An additional laptop represents a potential external or attacker device connected to the network.

---

## Devices and Their Roles

### Router

Routers connect different IP networks together and determine the best path for packets to reach their destination.

Examples used in the lab include Cisco 2911 routers.

### Switch

Switches connect devices within the same Local Area Network (LAN).

The Cisco 2960 switches in the topology connect PCs and other devices to the network.

### Firewall

A firewall controls traffic entering or leaving a network based on security rules.

Cisco ASA 5505 firewalls were included in the topology.

### End Devices

PCs and laptops represent users or hosts communicating across the network.
